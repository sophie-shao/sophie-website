About Me

the person beyond the projects

I'm a designer, developer, and storyteller who loves creating meaningful experiences at the intersection of people and technology.

My work is driven by curiosity — about people, their stories, and how technology can help us connect more deeply with the world around us. I believe the best designs come from understanding not just what people need, but what they care about.

When I'm not building products or designing interfaces, you'll find me hiking in the mountains, experimenting with watercolors, or getting lost in a good book.
